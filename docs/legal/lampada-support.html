Common questions

The widget shows nothing, or the same verse every day

Widgets are refreshed by iOS, not by the app. Remove the widget and add it again, then open Lampada once so it can rebuild its schedule. The verse changes at midnight, local time.

How do I set up the pause before the feed?

  1. Open Apple's Shortcuts app → Automation → +.
  2. Choose App, pick the social app, select Is Opened and Run Immediately.
  3. Add the action Pause with a verse from Lampada.
  4. Tap the blue App field inside that action and pick the same app again. This step is required — it is how Lampada knows where to send you back.

I tapped "Continue" and stayed in Lampada

The app field in the action is empty. Open the automation and pick the app inside the Pause with a verse action, as in step 4 above.

The Control Centre button is missing

It needs iOS 18 or newer. On iOS 17 the Lock Screen, Home Screen and StandBy widgets all work as usual.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you reinstalled the app, use Restore purchases on the subscription screen.

Which Bible translations are used?

The World English Bible, Luther 1912, Louis Segond 1910, Reina-Valera and the Russian Synodal translation. All are in the public domain, and all ship inside the app so it works offline.
